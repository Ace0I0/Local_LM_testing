ENVIRONMENTAL SENSOR GUIDANCE

Temperature readings are stored in degrees Celsius and may be presented in Fahrenheit.
Humidity readings are relative humidity percentages from zero to one hundred.
Online means the sensor node is connected to MQTT; it does not prove a measurement is fresh.
Offline means the location is unavailable.
A reading is stale when it has not been refreshed within the configured freshness window.
Artemis reports validated current values but does not infer comfort, health, danger, trends, or causes.
Artemis does not store historical sensor readings.
