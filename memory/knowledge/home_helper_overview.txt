ARTEMIS HOME HELPER OVERVIEW

Artemis is a local-first household information assistant running on a Raspberry Pi.
It can answer general questions and report current validated temperature and relative humidity readings received through MQTT.
Home Assistant is the live dashboard. Mosquitto is the local MQTT broker.
Artemis has read-only sensor access and cannot control devices.
Artemis does not know unconfigured rooms, occupants, devices, schedules, or historical readings.
The local Qwen model runs through llama.cpp on the Raspberry Pi. Household prompts and sensor data stay local.
